# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript, static build. The logo intro is a Remotion composition: the site plays it with `@remotion/player`, and the same composition renders to video with the Remotion CLI. Everything else is CSS transitions and the Web Animations API.

## Users

Women in Azerbaijan and its diaspora choosing an evening dress for an occasion: a wedding, an engagement, a gala, a dinner. They find the brand on Instagram, open the site on a phone, and want to see the cloth and the finishing up close before they write.

## Product Purpose

Arzu Almazzadeh is a couture label, made in Azerbaijan. The site shows the dresses and turns interest into a private consultation over WhatsApp. Success is a visitor sending that first WhatsApp message.

## Positioning

Bias-cut satin, lace and hand finishing, shown in the brand's own photographs. The cloth is the argument: close-ups of pintucks, lace, jacquard and pearl ties do the persuading.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Couture / made to order. No shop, no cart, no prices on the site.
- Primary action: WhatsApp consultation. The number is not provided yet; it lives in one constant (`src/lib/contact.ts`).
- Instagram: `@arzualmazzadeh` (confirmed by the owner).
- Languages: Azerbaijani by default, with English and Russian.
- Unknown and not to be invented: address, opening hours, lead times, prices, fabric composition, founding year, press, clients.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Arzu Almazzadeh. Mark: a round badge with an interlaced "AA" monogram in sand gold on bone.
- Hang tag (from the owner's photo): bone card, very large cropped geometric-sans letters "ARZU" in pale sand, "ALMAZZADEH" set small and spaced, and a "MADE IN AZERBAIJAN" label, on a braided gold cord.
- Intro, pinned by the owner: the logo draws itself from bottom to top, then the site opens like a page swiped up.
- Pinned feel: modern and elegant, not generic AI output.

## Evidence on Hand

- `assets-src/`: six photographs and the logo screenshot supplied by the owner (navy satin gown with pintucks and lace, the same gown in a flat-lay with shoes and earrings, ivory long-sleeve satin gown on a staircase, black slip gown with a lace back, bordeaux gown with ruffled armholes, olive jacquard with pearl ties and the hang tag).
- No testimonials, press, client names, prices or awards exist. None may be fabricated.

## Assumptions to confirm

- Fabric is described as "satin" (what the photos show), not "silk": composition is not confirmed.
- The look names on the site are working names written for the site.
- The four-step consultation-to-fitting process is a draft of how couture orders usually run.

## Product Principles

1. The cloth leads; the interface recedes.
2. Every part of the page is one tap from WhatsApp.
3. The brand's own tag and monogram are the visual system; nothing borrowed.
4. Never claim what the house has not confirmed.
