# Surface: home (single page)

Scope: the whole one-page site. Visitor mode: Persuade (with Experience in the collection).
Audience: women choosing an evening dress, arriving from Instagram on a phone.
Action: write on WhatsApp for a private consultation. Proof: the owner's photographs and close-ups of the cloth.
Constraints: AZ default, EN and RU; no prices, addresses, dates or fabric composition invented.

Direction roll: seed ff1484d1 ran degraded (no challengers). Grounded list, by resonance:
1 the brand's hang tag, 2 the swatch book / dyed cloth, 3 kelaghayi silk scarves, 4 the evening invitation,
5 the couture dress box, 6 Icheri Sheher limestone and carving, 7 the fitting room (tool rut).
Assigned #6 was declined on product-truth grounds: the brand already owns a world (tag + monogram) and
none of its photographs, tag or mark carry Old City stone; it would be a costume. Built: 1 fused with 2.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the brand's hang tag unfolded, and the collection is its swatch book. Refuses the cream-and-serif editorial hero with labels over headings and the grid of product cards.

OWN-WORLD: Bone card ground, sand-gold ink taken from the monogram, near-black text. One geometric sans (Jost) for everything, from giant cropped letters that run off the edge like the tag to small spaced capitals. A 1px gold cord line is the only ornament. Square corners. In the collection the whole page is dyed in each dress's colour.

STORY: See the cloth, believe the finishing, write on WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Intro: the monogram draws itself upward from the teardrop like ink, the name rises under it, then the bone sheet is swiped up. Underneath: the ivory gown photo tall on the right, "ARZU" in huge sand letters cropped by the bottom edge on the left, a one-line promise and the WhatsApp action above it, header with monogram, three links and AZ / EN / RU.

FORM: hang tag + swatch book (grounded #1 fused with #2), seed ff1484d1.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
